🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address, name, password
  • Profile information: Birth year, life goals, preferences
  • Content: Adventures, memories, photos, habits, bucket list items
  • Communications: Support requests, feedback

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: Device type, operating system, browser
  • Usage data: Features used, time spent, interactions
  • Log data: IP address, access times, error logs

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the LifeStack service
  • Process your subscription and payments
  • Send you service-related notifications
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Ensure the safety and security of our platform
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Image Processing and Content Safety

3.1 Automated Content Scanning

To maintain a safe platform, we use automated content moderation technology (Amazon Rekognition) to scan uploaded images for prohibited content. This scanning:

  • Occurs automatically when you upload images
  • Detects potentially prohibited content such as nudity, violence, or hate symbols
  • Does NOT use facial recognition to identify individuals
  • Does NOT share your images with third parties except as required by law

3.2 Content Review

If our automated systems flag content as potentially prohibited:

  • The content may be temporarily held for review
  • Our trust and safety team may review the flagged content
  • Content determined to violate our policies will be removed
  • You will be notified if content is removed (except in cases involving potential illegal activity)

3.3 Legal Reporting Obligations

We are legally required to report child sexual abuse material (CSAM) to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). Such reports include:

  • The content itself
  • Associated user account information
  • IP addresses and timestamps
  • Any other legally required information

We may also report other illegal content to law enforcement as required or permitted by law.

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers: AWS (hosting), payment processors
  • Friends: Only what you explicitly choose to share
  • Legal authorities: When required by law or to report illegal content

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored securely on Amazon Web Services (AWS)
  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
  • We use industry-standard security practices
  • Access to user data is strictly limited and logged

6. Data Retention

  • Active account data: Retained while your account is active
  • Deleted account data: Removed within 30 days of account deletion
  • Flagged content: May be retained for up to 90 days for review
  • Violation records: Retained for 7 years for legal compliance
  • Reported content: Preserved as required by law enforcement

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and data
  • Export: Download your data (Plus/Pro plans)
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications

8. Children's Privacy

LifeStack is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If we discover such information has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

9. International Users

Your data is processed and stored in the United States. By using LifeStack, you consent to the transfer of your data to the United States.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notification.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at: privacy@lifestack.app
