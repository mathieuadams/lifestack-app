# Fieldbook Brand Guide

## Brand Core
- Brand name: `Fieldbook`
- Tagline: `Plan slowly. Share warmly. Keep everything.`
- Positioning: The emotional middle ground between planning and remembering.
- Core message: `This is mine. This is real. This is worth keeping.`

## Brand Pillars
- Intentional by Design
  Design for calm decisions, not urgency.
- Analog Soul / Digital Form
  Keep the tactile feeling of a personal journal in a digital product.
- Intimate, Not Social
  Built for trusted people, not audiences.
- Keepworthy
  Make memories and plans durable, organized, and retrievable.

## Voice and Tone
- Warm
  Human, personal, and kind.
- Unhurried
  No hype, no pressure language, no fear-of-missing-out cues.
- Grounded
  Specific and clear. Real language over marketing language.
- Encouraging
  Supportive prompts and next steps, never guilt-driven.

## We Say / We Never Say
| We Say | We Never Say |
|---|---|
| Keep this memory | Post content |
| Invite people you trust | Grow your audience |
| Your fieldbook | Your profile feed |
| Plan your trip | Optimize your productivity stack |
| Keep what matters | Never miss out |
| Quietly shared | Viral |
| Worth keeping | Disposable content |

## Vocabulary Guidelines
- Prefer: `fieldbook`, `trip`, `memory`, `keep`, `share with contributors`, `timeline`, `yearly`, `map`, `private`.
- Avoid: `social`, `followers`, `engagement`, `viral`, `gamification`, `streak anxiety`, `content`.

## Audience Messages
- For planners
  Plan with intention, then let the trip breathe.
- For memory keepers
  Keep moments in context, not as scattered uploads.
- For sharers
  Share with people you care about, not with everyone.
- For the overwhelmed
  One calm place for plans, notes, photos, and places.

## What Fieldbook Is Not
- Not a social network.
- Not an attention trap.
- Not built around infinite feeds or vanity metrics.
- Not disposable design.
